# mood-diary
一款集成 AI 情绪分析的全栈心理健康管理应用，用户可通过写日记记录情绪，系统自动量化情绪数据并可视化，同时提供 AI 对话、情绪胶囊、匿名社区等功能。（An AI-powered full-stack mental health management app that lets users track emotions through journaling, automatically quantifies and visualizes mood data, and offers AI chat, emotion capsules, and an anonymous community.）
